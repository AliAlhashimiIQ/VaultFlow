🔒 VaultFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Summary: VaultFlow stores ALL your data locally on your device. We do NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal or financial information.

1. Data Collection

VaultFlow is a fully offline-first personal finance tracker. The app does not collect any personal data, financial data, or usage analytics. All information you enter — including transactions, categories, savings goals, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device.

2. Data Storage

All data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This data is:

  • Never transmitted to any server
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Never used for advertising purposes
  • Only accessible by the VaultFlow app on your device

3. Data Export

VaultFlow offers optional data export features (JSON backup and CSV ledger). When you export data, the files are created in your device's temporary storage and shared via your chosen method (email, cloud storage, etc.). VaultFlow does not control what happens to exported files after they leave the app.

4. Permissions

VaultFlow requests no special permissions. It does not access your camera, contacts, location, microphone, or any other sensitive device features.

5. Third-Party Services

VaultFlow does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the Settings screen using the "Reset All Data" option. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all data permanently.

7. Children's Privacy

VaultFlow does not knowingly collect data from children. The app is a general-purpose finance tracker and does not target children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: vaultflow.app@gmail.com
